# DeFi Basics

DeFi means decentralized finance: financial tools built with smart contracts. Examples include decentralized exchanges, lending markets, and liquidity pools.

Key ideas:
- A DEX lets users trade without a centralized order book.
- Liquidity providers deposit assets into pools.
- Smart contract bugs, depegs, governance attacks, and liquidation risk matter.
- High yield often comes with high risk.

DeFi is powerful, but users should treat every protocol as something to research carefully.

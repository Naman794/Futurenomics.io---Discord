# Bitcoin Basics

Bitcoin is a decentralized digital money system. It lets people transfer value over the internet without relying on a bank or payment company to approve each transaction.

Key ideas:
- Bitcoin has a fixed supply schedule with a maximum of 21 million BTC.
- Transactions are grouped into blocks and secured by miners using proof of work.
- The network is public, but users are represented by addresses rather than real names.
- Holding Bitcoin safely requires understanding wallets, seed phrases, and private keys.

Bitcoin is volatile. Learn the technology and risks before making any financial decision.

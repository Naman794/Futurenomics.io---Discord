# Wallets and Self-Custody

A crypto wallet stores keys, not coins. The assets are recorded on the blockchain; your private key proves that you can move them.

Key ideas:
- A public address can receive funds.
- A private key or seed phrase controls funds.
- Never share your seed phrase.
- Hardware wallets can reduce online attack risk.
- Test with small transfers before moving meaningful value.

Self-custody gives control, but it also gives responsibility.
